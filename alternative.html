RESEARCH

Research interests

Rotate to explore

PRIMARY

Mechanistic
interpretability

I study the mechanistic reasons why foundation models can do some things and cannot do others, and how we can use their nature to guide their behavior.

Understanding these mechanisms can make models more interpretable, controllable, and trustworthy — and offer new perspectives for exploring AI.

A CLOSER LOOK

Representation
geometry

My particular focus is representation geometry and representation learning: the structures inside models that give rise to their behavior.

I am especially interested in the geometrical features of latent spaces and ways to move beyond the Linear Representation Hypothesis.

SECONDARY

Trustworthy AI

I investigate the safety, fairness, and reliability of LLMs: defending against jailbreak attacks, detecting and mitigating social biases within model representations, and improving the robustness of machine-generated text detection.
